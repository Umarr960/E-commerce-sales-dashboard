🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Microsoft Power BI
📊 Project Overview

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize e-commerce business performance.
The dashboard provides meaningful insights into sales, revenue, profit, customers, orders, and product performance through interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and visualizations.
It helps business users understand sales trends, identify top-performing products and categories, analyze customer behavior, and make data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
Track key business KPIs such as Sales, Profit, Orders, and Quantity Sold.
Understand revenue and profit trends over time.
Identify top-performing products and categories.
Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
Compare sales performance across different regions.
Identify profitable and underperforming products.
Provide an interactive and user-friendly business intelligence dashboard.
